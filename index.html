About

I am a Master's student at the School of Computer Science, Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU). I am a member of the APEX Lab, privileged to be advised by Prof. Yong Yu and Prof. Weinan Zhang. Prior to this, I obtained my Bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Technology from SJTU in 2024.

My research focuses on foundation model post-training and code intelligence. Specifically:

  • Agentic Coding Post-training Training code agents with reinforcement learning and execution feedback in realistic software environments, so that they can plan, navigate large repositories, and complete long-horizon software tasks.
  • Multi-Agent Systems Improving how frontier models collaborate, delegate, and aggregate findings inside multi-agent harnesses, so that agent teams can solve complex long-horizon problems beyond the reach of a single agent.
  • Test-time Scaling of LLMs Decoupling planning from execution: spending extra inference compute on searching and refining natural-language thoughts and plans rather than raw code or actions, with execution feedback as verification.

I'm always happy to connect — feel free to reach out to discuss research or collaborations at WeimingZhang_2020[at]sjtu[dot]edu[dot]cn.
